    <h3>SwarmRL</h3>
    <hr/>
    <p>

        bridging RL research and statistical-physics simulation. Developed at the
        <a href="https://www.icp.uni-stuttgart.de/" target="_blank">ICP Stuttgart</a> as part of
        our group's research on intelligent active matter.
