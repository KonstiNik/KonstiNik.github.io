abstract: >-
  Curricula can reduce pretraining costs, but no principle predicts in advance when one will help.
  On the Random Hierarchy Model, a generative model of hierarchically compositional data, a curriculum
  that reweights rather than restricts the training data can speed training up (by up to 2.1x) or slow
  it down (by up to 10x), and its advantage peaks long after the curriculum has ended. Both follow from
  the staircase shape of the loss curve, and we derive a criterion from the data model and the training
  budget alone that predicts whether a curriculum will help or hurt.
