abstract: >-
  As language models grow larger, they learn from progressively weaker signals — patterns
  in the data too faint for smaller models to pick up. We introduce <em>spectral position</em>,
  a measure that reads off the strength of the signal a model is currently learning from
  and can be applied at scale, and show that scaling drives progress into the spectral tail.
